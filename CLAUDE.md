# Global Guidelines for Claude

## Text Style & Anti-Watermark Guidelines (watermarks-remover)

Whenever writing or editing user-facing prose, reports, documentation, .md, or .docx files:

1. **Anti-Watermark & Hygiene**:
   - Follow the installed skills `clean-user-facing-text` and `remove-ai-marks` (from https://github.com/guillaumemeyer/watermarks-remover).
   - Strip invisible Unicode characters, zero-width spaces, exotic space separators, and Unicode homoglyphs.
   - Write cleanly and naturally without artificial AI cadences or statistical watermarks.

2. **No Em-Dashes**:
   - Do NOT use em-dashes (`—`). Use standard hyphens (`-`) or restructure sentences.

3. **Human Tone & Ukrainian Academic Style**:
   - Use concise, academic Ukrainian for university/technical work.
   - Focus on key substance without boilerplate filler or clichés.
   - Sound like a human practitioner: direct, structured, concrete.
