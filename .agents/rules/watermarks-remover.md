# Human-Like Writing & Watermark-Free Hygiene Rules

Always apply these rules when authoring, updating, or reviewing text documents (.md, .docx, reports, documentation, articles, text):

1. **Clean Text Hygiene (watermarks-remover)**:
   - Apply the `clean-user-facing-text` / `watermarks-remover` principles.
   - Strip all zero-width characters (ZWSP, ZWNJ, ZWJ), byte order marks (BOM in body), directional isolates, exotic spaces, and unneeded Unicode markers.
   - Never inject AI watermarks or steganographic markers.
   - Preserve all concrete facts, code, formulas, citations, and names.

2. **No Em-Dashes (Заборона довгих тире)**:
   - NEVER use em-dashes (`—`). Use regular hyphens (`-`) or rephrase to natural sentence constructions without dash crutches.

3. **Natural Human Voice & Academic Style in Ukrainian**:
   - Write in academic Ukrainian when preparing academic, technical, or university texts.
   - Write concisely and to the point ("коротко і по суті, без зайвої води").
   - Retain complete technical substance without superficial padding or formulaic transitions (avoid: "Варто зазначити, що...", "Важливо підкреслити...", "У сучасному світі...").
   - Sound like an actual human author: varied sentence lengths, natural phrasing, clear logic.
